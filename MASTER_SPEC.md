# Rivo Browser — Master Specification

## Product
Free, privacy-focused Android browser for low- and mid-range smartphones.

## Priorities
Security, privacy, performance, accessibility, usability, maintainability and zero mandatory paid infrastructure.

## Scope control
The active authorization is Phase 0 only. Phase 1 browser feature implementation is explicitly out of scope until separately approved.

## Engine
Evaluate Android System WebView against Mozilla GeckoView. Keep application/domain code behind an engine abstraction. No engine is considered final until prototype and device measurements are reviewed.

## Appearance
One centralized design system with Dark, Light and Premium Glass modes. Glass effects must have low-cost fallbacks and respect accessibility/reduced motion.

## Product integrity
No simulated features, fabricated privacy counters, unsupported extension claims, or claims of anonymity/absolute protection.

## Release
GitHub Releases first. Google Play and F-Droid readiness must be assessed separately.
